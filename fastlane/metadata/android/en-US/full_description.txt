Spartial Music is an open-source Android music player with a dark, glassy interface.

Play the music you own, and stream what is legal to stream. Spartial Music reads
audio already on your device, streams Creative Commons music from Jamendo, and
connects to YouTube Music. Nothing is scraped, extracted or downloaded from
YouTube.

FEATURES
- Five floating tabs: Home, Trending, Suggestions, Library and Search
- Full-bleed Home hero, recently played, continue playing and daily shelves
- Now Playing with controls, synced lyrics and queue modes
- Artwork-tinted backgrounds on Now Playing, Playlist, Album and Artist pages
- Playlists, pins, likes, recents, artists and local library search
- Queue reorder, sleep timer, playback speed and skip silence
- Synced lyrics from local .lrc files
- Media notification, lock screen controls and optional Cast
- Settings for appearance, playback, lyrics, storage and diagnostics

PRIVACY
No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Your library never leaves
the device. Crash reports are written locally and are never uploaded.

SOURCES
- YouTube Music through the unofficial InnerTube client (default source)
- Official YouTube Data API v3 provider and embedded player (code included)
- Local device files through MediaStore
- Jamendo (Creative Commons)

The unofficial YouTube Music source is outside the YouTube API Services Terms and
can break when YouTube changes its private endpoints. Spartial Music never
downloads, caches or re-encodes YouTube media and never skips ads.

LICENCE
GPL-3.0-or-later. No album art, artist names or audio from third parties is
bundled. Spartial Music is not affiliated with YouTube, Google, Jamendo or any
streaming service.
