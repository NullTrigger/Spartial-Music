Nodes is an open-source music player for Android with a dark, glassy interface.

Play the music you own, and stream what is legal to stream. Nodes reads audio
already on your device, streams Creative Commons music from Jamendo, and browses
YouTube through its official Data API with playback in the official embedded
player. Nothing is scraped, extracted or downloaded from YouTube.

FEATURES
- Local library via MediaStore, with no copies or uploads
- Creative Commons streaming from Jamendo, credited per track
- Official YouTube browsing: search, charts, playlists
- Hero carousel home screen with blurred glass surfaces
- Likes, playlists, play history and search history stored on device
- Synced lyrics from local .lrc files
- Queue, seek, media notification, lock screen controls
- Settings for sources, audio and cache

PRIVACY
No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Your library never leaves
the device. Optional Google Sign-In only reads your own YouTube playlists with the
youtube.readonly scope, and the token is kept in memory for the session.

SOURCES
- Local device files
- Jamendo (Creative Commons)
- YouTube (official API; bring your own API key - see YOUTUBE.md)
