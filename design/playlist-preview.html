A · Editorial hero

Borrows the Home hero's composition and gives a playlist the identity it currently lacks. The hero scrolls away and the title collapses into the header, so long playlists still browse well.

  • Needs a playlist aggregate: total runtime, source name
  • Hero gradient derived from the first track's artwork (or GeneratedArt seed)
  • Numbered rows with the active track in teal + equaliser
  • Shuffle and Like become reachable instead of hidden
Effort · medium

B · Cover + glass ledger

The calmest of the four. One glass slab holds the queue, and a bottom action bar keeps "Play all" reachable without a floating button covering a row.

  • Cover card = generated art for the playlist, no new assets
  • Active row gets a teal rail + tinted wash, no layout jump
  • Bottom bar mirrors the MiniPlayer's glass treatment
  • Works unchanged for YouTube playlists without cover art
Effort · medium

C · Mosaic poster

The most distinctive, and the most opinionated: the playlist's first four tracks are its cover, and the list becomes typographic rather than thumbnail-driven. Best for a curated, editorial feel.

  • Mosaic from four GeneratedArt tiles, no image downloads
  • Serif display title matches the app's existing serif face
  • No row artwork, so rows are taller and easier to scan
  • Heaviest change: new header layout and a FAB in the list area
Effort · larger
Mockup only — nothing here is wired into the app yet. All cover art is CSS gradient placeholder art derived from ArtAccents; track titles are the invented entries in DemoCatalog.kt.
